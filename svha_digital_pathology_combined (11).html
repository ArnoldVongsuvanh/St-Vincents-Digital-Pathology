Validation Protocol

Adapted from the UK Royal College of Pathologists, College of American Pathologists (CAP), and University Health Network protocols. Click each phase to expand the detailed requirements.

📋

Validation is mandatory before primary diagnosis sign-out on digital slides. In Australia, NATA accreditation requirements apply. The dual-track period (concurrent glass + digital review) typically runs 3–12 months. Each pathologist self-determines readiness. Bruce 2024 Montezuma 2022

Technical Scanner Validation

Confirm scanner meets specifications: resolution (≥20× / 0.5 µm/pixel), scan time, jamming rate, barcode read accuracy. Vendor testing with real SydPath slides before purchase commitment. DigiPatICS 2022

Phase 1 — Archive Case Validation (Per Pathologist)

15–20 curated archive cases per pathologist including known diagnostic pitfalls (granulomas, H. pylori, mitoses, fungi). Compare digital to glass blind — concordance must meet institutional threshold. Takes 1–2 months in practice. Montezuma 2022 Bruce 2024

Phase 2 — Prospective Dual-Track Validation

Pathologists review routine cases both digitally and on glass simultaneously. Sign-out only on glass until sufficient confidence. A percentage of daily workload (not all cases) reviewed in dual mode. Duration varies per pathologist. Montezuma 2022

IHC & Cytology-Specific Validation

Separate validation sets required for IHC stains, frozen sections, and cytology (if adopted). Colour fidelity of chromogens (DAB, AEC) must be confirmed on calibrated monitors. DigiPatICS 2022 Bruce 2024

Continuous Quality Validation

Post go-live: periodic scanner performance audits, image quality sampling (5–10% of daily cases), pathologist re-scan request tracking, and ISO 15189 / NATA continuous compliance. CAP guidelines recommend ongoing performance assessment. DigiPatICS 2022 UHN 2025

AI Algorithm Validation

Each AI tool requires independent clinical validation in the local pathology context. TGA regulatory pathway (IVD classification) applies in Australia. Analogous to FDA 510(k) pathway. Paige Prostate is the first FDA-cleared AI pathology product. Montezuma 2022 Bruce 2024
